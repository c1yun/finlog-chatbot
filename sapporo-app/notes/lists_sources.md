## Tax-free (refund method). Applies to a March 2027 trip
- Purchases from 2026-11-01: the traveler pays the tax-inclusive price. The threshold is 5,000 yen or more (tax excluded) per store per day. A customs check at a terminal must happen within 90 days of purchase, before checking bags, and checked luggage can't be retrieved for it. If one item from a receipt is missing, the whole receipt is ineligible. Food, drink and cosmetics used in Japan are excluded. The shop or its agent pays the refund, and the method isn't fixed. VJW can be used in designated Wi-Fi areas at major airports. https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/tax-free/page01_000001_00028.html
- Store-side outline. Separate shipping (別送) was abolished on 2025-03-31. https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/tax-free/page01_000001_00019.html
- New Chitose is listed among the airports with a VJW procedure Wi-Fi area in the international departures lobby. This comes from a search snippet of a Tourism Agency PDF, and I couldn't extract the PDF text. https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/tax-free/content/001978487.pdf
- Cash Japan refund service at 6 airports, including New Chitose, from 11/1 (news). https://www.aviationwire.jp/?p=352751

## Baggage and customs

- Medicines are an exception to the liquids rule. https://www.kansai-airport.or.jp/flight/route/liquid
- Ice packs over 100 ml aren't allowed in the cabin even when frozen (secondary). https://tour.l-tike.com/contents/media/article20260216.html
- Power banks: from 2026-04-24, Japan allows up to 2, each 160 Wh or less, carry-on only. No charging or use on board, and not in overhead bins. https://www.japan.travel/ko/kr/topic/2026nyeon-4weol-24il-jst-buteo-sihaengdoeneun-ilbon-ginae-banib-bojobaeteori-gyujeong/
- Korea: from 2026-04-20, 2 per person, with charging and use banned. https://www.smarttoday.co.kr/ko-kr/articles/106261
- Hand warmers: there are reports that Korean airports have barred them from checked bags since January 2026 (secondary, unconfirmed). https://www.funliday.com/posts/korea-hand-warmer-rule/ . Iron-powder disposable warmers are generally allowed in the cabin (secondary). https://monomoney-living.com/kairo-airplane/ . The JSON says to carry them on board.
- Korea duty-free: 800 USD. Alcohol has a 2 L and 400 USD cap, with the bottle limit removed in March 2025. Perfume is limited to 100 ml. https://www.taxwatch.co.kr/article/tax/2025/09/19/0002
- Korea: meat products such as ham, sausage and jerky are banned, with fines up to 10 million KRW. https://www.segye.com/newsView/20260723506675 . There's no official ruling on meat-containing retort curry, so the JSON is cautious.
- Medicines into Japan: prescription drugs up to a 1-month supply and OTC up to 2 months without a certificate. https://www.mhlw.go.jp/english/topics/import/index.html , https://www.miami.us.emb-japan.go.jp/en/medical.html
- Pseudoephedrine over 10% is a stimulant raw material and is banned. Codeine over 1% is treated as a narcotic. https://www.au.emb-japan.go.jp/pdf/Yakkan_Shoumei_Forms.pdf

## Health
- Ministry of the Environment onsen cautions. Drink a cup of water before and after. Avoid bathing right after meals or alcohol. The elderly and people with hypertension should avoid 42°C or hotter. The elderly shouldn't bathe alone. Get out slowly. https://www.env.go.jp/content/900453265.pdf (English text extracted locally)
- Consumer Affairs Agency: 41°C or lower, up to 10 minutes, don't stand up suddenly. https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/consumer_safety/caution/caution_009/ (via search) , https://www.city.osaka.lg.jp/lnet/cmsfiles/contents/0000534/534982/163_1221.pdf
- Onsen manners: no swimsuits, keep towels out of the water, tattoos refused (small ones can be covered), wash first. https://www.mlit.go.jp/tagengo-db/en/H30-00618.html
- Chilblains are likely at 3-5°C with a day-night swing of 10°C or more, late autumn to early spring. Keep hands and feet dry and covered. https://fdoc.jp/byouki-scope/features/chilblains-cause/
