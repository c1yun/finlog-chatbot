## 1. AI-cloud build-out timeline (GPUs, contracts, sites, ARR guidance)

### Takeaway
IREN went from ~1,900 GPUs/"AI cloud as side business" in early 2025 to a ~150,000-GPU order book, $4B of contracted 2026 ARR and ~$1B operating ARR by Aug 2026, anchored by the Nov-2025 $9.7B Microsoft contract (Childress Horizon 1–4) and the May-2026 $3.4B NVIDIA contract + 5GW partnership. Each large contract produced a double-digit up-day; each delivery/acceptance milestone produced +5–6%.
