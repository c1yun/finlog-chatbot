### Inferences
- With a typical daily range of 6–8% and beta ≈ 2.5–4, a "normal" IREN session spans roughly ±$2.5–3 at a $35–40 price; the first hour (22:30–23:30 KST) is statistically the most likely hour to print the day's low (c), so Korean traders buying in the 17:00–22:30 KST pre-market on a gap-up are frequently filled above the eventual session low.
- The two best-documented large gaps split cleanly: news that arrives pre-market and is "digestible" (Microsoft deal) faded ~55% of its peak by the close; a gap that rides a sector-wide risk-on reversal (Oct 13 2025) held. This matches the generic "gap > ATR tends to fade / gap with sector confirmation tends to hold" heuristic, but n=2.
- Pre-market percentage moves reported by Benzinga (+2.9% to +6.1%) are small relative to the 7–8% ATR, so pre-market prints are not reliable indicators of the regular-session direction for IREN.
